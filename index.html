Indonesian Heroes Who Can Inspire Young People

Raden Ajeng Kartini

21 April 1879 – 17 September 1904

R.A. Kartini is a female hero from Java and has officially been recognized as a national hero of Indonesia. She is known as a determined heroic figure who never gave up fighting for women's rights, specifically for the women's rights in Java. She primarily focused on advocating for girls' and women's rights for education in her area. Besides that, she also established Kartini Schools in Java and other areas.

From this heroic figure, young people can learn to never give up fighting for something that should be theirs. We can see this by how R.A. Kartini saw the unfairness of the existing patriarchy, and fought against it, because she knew that's what her and other girls and women deserved. From this heroic figure, young people can also learn to always have an undying determination at the things they do. R.A. Kartini was determined enough to push through everything, just so she could gain basic education rights for girls and women. Maybe not all young people are trying to do something as big as what R.A. Kartini did, but nevertheless, young people can still learn from R.A. Kartini's undying determination.

Sutomo

3 October 1920 – 7 October 1981

Sutomo, or more well known as Bung Tomo, is a famous Indonesian heroic figure and has officially been recognized as a national hero of Indonesia. He played a central role in the Battle of Surabaya, because he gave rousing speeches to the people that were fighting in the battle. He gave people motivational speeches through his radio broadcasts, and people said that the emotions that were present in his voice truly boosted the morale of the people of Surabaya. He is quite a popular national hero, and he has became inspiration to many people.

Bung Tomo is mainly popular because of his quote, "MERDEKA ATAU MATI!" or, "FREEDOM OR DEATH!" in English. That quote really shows how important Indonesia's independence was and still is. This can teach young people to always fight for their beloved country, Indonesia, because from how people reacted to Bung Tomo's famous quote, we can see how tired they were back then. They all agreed to continue to fight for freedom, since collonization was, in fact, a hard thing to go through. So, in short, Bung Tomo can inspire young people to always fight for freedom, and settle for nothing less.

Cut Nyak Dhien

1848 – 6 November 1908

Cut Nyak Dhien is a female hero from Aceh and has been officially recognized as a national hero of Indonesia. She played an important role in the Aceh War against the Dutch East Indies colonial government, as she was the leader in said war. She fought and worked hard to protect her region, even if the two men she married died along the way. She was a good leader and a good soldier, and that's what makes her inspirational.

Cut Nyak Dhien is especially inspirational in the eyes of girls and women, since she was a woman and a soldier, which wasn't very common or looked up to at the time. She can be very inspirational specifically towards women and girls in the younger generation, since her story shows that despite social expectations, you can go against it and do what you do best. Her story shows that you can fight society's norms, and you can, in fact, make people proud of you, despite you breaking the norms in society. Her story shows that women and girls in society don't have to act fragile, dainty, and sweet all the time, and this can teach the girls and women in the younger generation to be themselves, as long as they're still doing good.
